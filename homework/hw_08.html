January 6, 2021

In the 2020 US presidential election, Donald Trump (the incumbent) lost to the Democrat candidate Joe Biden. After the election, belief spread among some of Trump’s supporters that the election had been stolen, and Trump himself insisted that he had won.

On January 6, 2021, a large group of Trump supporters attempted to prevent Congress from certifying the election results, and thereby keep Trump in power. Thousands of people stormed the Capitol building, and there were clashes with police officers and damage done to the building and offices of members of Congress. Several deaths and many injuries resulted.

In the aftermath, 1424 individuals were charged with federal crimes. The data in this assignment comes from a research study investigating what “local political, economic, and social conditions of the communities” might explain participation in January 6.

Data

Each row in the dataset represents one county in the US, and the main variable of interest is the number of individuals in each county criminally charged for participation in January 6. The other variables record social, political, and economic information about the counties.

The data can be loaded into R with:

library(tidyverse)

jan_6 <- read_csv("https://sta712-f26.github.io/homework/jan_6.csv")

Your task
